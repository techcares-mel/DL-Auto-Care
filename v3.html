About Us

We Take Pride

DL Auto Care is a family-owned smash and mechanical repair shop based in Braybrook, Melbourne. With over a decade of hands-on experience, we've built our reputation on honest advice, quality workmanship, and making sure every customer leaves satisfied.

Whether it's a small dent, a full body respray, or a complex engine repair — we treat every job with the same care and attention. All makes and models, from daily drivers to prestige vehicles.
